# Skill 3 — Angle Measure and Types

**Skill ID:** `geo-u1-angle-measure`
**Difficulty band:** 1
**Prereqs:** geo-u1-point-line-plane

---

## Concept

An **angle** is formed by two rays sharing a common endpoint. That shared endpoint is the **vertex**; the two rays are the **sides** of the angle. We measure the "opening" between the sides in **degrees** — a full rotation is 360°.

**Naming.** Angle *ABC* (written $\angle ABC$) means the angle whose vertex is *B* — and the middle letter is always the vertex. The first and last letters name a point on each side. We can also write just $\angle B$ if there's no ambiguity. Stay alert: $\angle ABC$ and $\angle CBA$ are the same angle; $\angle ACB$ is a different angle entirely.

**Measure.** The measure of $\angle ABC$ is written $m\angle ABC$. Just like with segments, the angle itself is a *shape* and its measure is a *number*. $\angle ABC \cong \angle DEF$ means the shapes are congruent; $m\angle ABC = m\angle DEF$ means the numbers are equal.

**Four classifications by measure:**

- **Acute:** $0° < m\angle < 90°$
- **Right:** $m\angle = 90°$ (mark with a small square at the vertex)
- **Obtuse:** $90° < m\angle < 180°$
- **Straight:** $m\angle = 180°$ (the two rays form a line)

**Angle Addition Postulate.** If point *D* lies in the interior of $\angle ABC$, then $m\angle ABD + m\angle DBC = m\angle ABC$. Same idea as segment addition, just rotated.

## Manipulate (interactive)

You'll see rays $\overrightarrow{BA}$ and $\overrightarrow{BC}$ sharing vertex *B*. Point *A* is fixed; point *C* is draggable. **Drag *C* until $m\angle ABC = 90°$.**

The angle measure displays live. When you hit 90° (within a degree of slop), the right-angle square appears and you've nailed it.

*Why this matters:* "right angle" is the single most-used measurement in geometry. If you can recognize one by eye, you'll work faster on every problem.

## Worked Example

> Ray $\overrightarrow{BD}$ is in the interior of $\angle ABC$. If $m\angle ABD = 35°$ and $m\angle DBC = 47°$, find $m\angle ABC$.

**Step 1.** Read the picture. *D* is inside $\angle ABC$, so the Angle Addition Postulate applies.

**Step 2.** Write the postulate: $m\angle ABD + m\angle DBC = m\angle ABC$.

**Step 3.** Substitute: $35° + 47° = m\angle ABC$.

**Step 4.** Add: $m\angle ABC = 82°$.

**Step 5.** Classify. $82°$ is between $0°$ and $90°$, so this is an **acute** angle.

**Answer:** $m\angle ABC = 82°$, acute.

## Reflection

An angle is just two rays with a shared vertex. Four classifications by measure. One postulate for adding measures of adjacent angles.

The notation trap to remember: $m\angle ABC$ is a number; $\angle ABC$ is a shape. The middle letter is always the vertex. And $\angle ABC = \angle CBA$ but $\angle ABC \neq \angle ACB$.
